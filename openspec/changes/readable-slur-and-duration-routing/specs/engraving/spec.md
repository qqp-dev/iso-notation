## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Local visible phrase attachment
Source slurs SHALL retain their independent endpoint owners and explicit source sides while remaining visibly attached to the owning gesture. Their placement SHALL consider neighboring curves together. An unavailable local route SHALL be diagnosed instead of silently treating a distant unrelated lane as a successful attachment.

#### Scenario: Crowded independently voiced phrase
- **WHEN** No13 printed35–36 / internal38–40 presents concurrent RH phrases and LH B–6–4 / B–4 slurs
- **THEN** both LH slurs remain associated with their own branches in the lower musical gesture, every RH phrase survives, and all source sides, notes and clocks are preserved

### Requirement: Complete duration mark clearance
Independent duration marks SHALL retain their complete written glyphs and clear foreign rhythm ink. Placement, paint, physical inventories and diagnostics SHALL agree about the whole flag or ring and its stem; clearing the stem alone SHALL NOT establish clearance.

#### Scenario: Flag crossing two foreign levels
- **WHEN** the final RH4 in No13's 4–3–4 gesture has a solo flag alongside another voice's two beams
- **THEN** the complete flag remains readable without merging with either foreign beam, and the defect is detected if that clearance is removed

#### Scenario: Additional subdivision
- **WHEN** the same physical relationship carries a shorter written value with two flags
- **THEN** both flags receive complete clearance without altering pitch, onset, source voice or written value

### Requirement: Independent unequal-value shared-head branches
An unequal-value shared head SHALL retain all written duration and continuation obligations. The comparison SHALL expose shorter and longer bare-branch terminals without deleting a value, inventing an attack, or using an ordinary slur as a pitch tie. Neither candidate SHALL be treated as an operator-selected presentation before review.

#### Scenario: Shared B eighth and quarter
- **WHEN** No13's B has an eighth in the moving B–6–4 voice and a quarter before4 in the other voice
- **THEN** both shorter and longer bare-branch candidates retain one compatible attack, both values and distinct terminals; a repaired-phrasing baseline retains the original B, and each slur retains its own source owners through the final shared E

### Requirement: Honest comparison and validation
The studio SHALL expose the retained aligned reading, repaired-phrasing baseline and two shared-head variants with truthful source/window cues. The shared rules SHALL be audited on canonical contexts; defaults remain unchanged for this candidate review. Retained history, merged code, served ink and musician acceptance SHALL remain distinguishable.
