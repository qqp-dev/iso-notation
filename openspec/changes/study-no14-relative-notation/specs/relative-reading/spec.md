# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Let a pianist read exact source music through visible played-note references and reusable interval digits, retaining recoverable source identity and honest experimental status.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Source-preserving group-local reading
The current candidate SHALL retain source pitch/register geometry and every temporal, hand, voice, expression and repeat obligation. An underlined played anchor SHALL display its absolute digit; interiors and explicitly continuing notes SHALL display relative0-B within their identified reading context. The terminal LH note SHALL continue its preceding LH reference without a new anchor. The two standalone LH dotted quarters48 SHALL retain their existing absolute treatment. Mode, reference, octave and owner/group metadata SHALL recover every original pitch and shared obligation exactly. Concurrent groups SHALL retain independent references.

#### Scenario: Concurrent gestures
- **WHEN** a melodic figure establishes its own reference while accompaniment continues
- **THEN** only that figure's members use its reference and accompaniment retains its own reading mode/reference

### Requirement: Source-derived No14 gestures
The candidate SHALL identify No14's ordinary LH/RH three-eighth figures, genuinely continuous cross-hand ascending arpeggio, and held/short RH shared-head figures from actual source rhythm/onset/hand membership. Standalone LH dotted quarters48 SHALL retain their existing absolute treatment; the terminal LH singleton SHALL continue the preceding LH group's reference and display0. Group references SHALL apply only to their members and explicit continuations and SHALL decode identically on repeat traversals; no bar-global carry or maximum-one-anchor-per-bar constraint SHALL govern the active candidate.

#### Scenario: Shared held and moving head
- **WHEN** a held RH note aliases the first short note of its figure
- **THEN** the common painted head retains both written owners, the absolute start and the same local group reference

#### Scenario: Final note continues the reference
- **WHEN** the terminal LH note follows its established7 reference without a new anchor
- **THEN** it displays relative0 with no underline, retains its source pitch/register and duration, and does not take the independent RH group's reference

### Requirement: Relative-parity placement
Parity columns within simultaneous relative chord groups SHALL follow relative pitch-class parity. An absolute printed anchor SHALL occupy relative0's column. Successive arpeggio attacks SHALL retain rhythmic temporal positions without relative-parity rail offsets. Exact source register, hand and duration geometry SHALL remain intact; changing text alone SHALL NOT be claimed as simultaneous relative-parity placement.

#### Scenario: Odd absolute anchor
- **WHEN** the reference pitch is odd
- **THEN** its simultaneous chord column assignments use interval parity rather than the original absolute parity

#### Scenario: Successive arpeggio attacks
- **WHEN** relative interval parity changes between successive attacks
- **THEN** parity SHALL NOT move those attacks sideways or shorten/lengthen their rhythmic spacing

### Requirement: Exact explicit readability target
The retained historical baseline SHALL minimize a declared finite additive readability cost distinguishing useful relative patterns, charging resets and admitting arbitrary content. It SHALL disclose target, itinerary and measured solver runtime without harmonic-root or universal human-optimal claims.

#### Scenario: Exhaustive small comparison
- **WHEN** a tiny score permits exhaustive legal anchor enumeration
- **THEN** the solver returns the same minimum cost with causal and per-bar constraints intact

### Requirement: Honest complete-page experiment
The candidate SHALL show all64 written bars as real-engine pages in the existing studio, use compound3+3 pulse independently of duration arithmetic and audit actual displayed glyph/anchor-mark bounds and owners. Candidate preparation SHALL preserve Reference/default/PDF/history/services and use native DEV review without a production build or PDF. Following the operator's explicit Round81 landing request, only the No14 published Reference/home sheet and matching export SHALL adopt the accepted three-page profile; earlier absolute profiles and other works SHALL remain intact. Compiled5189/5190 and live5175 SHALL remain unchanged. Historical bracket review builds remain separate snapshots.

#### Scenario: Complete baseline review
- **WHEN** the operator opens the baseline
- **THEN** all written obligations are present, underlined anchors belong to played heads, grouped interiors and the final continuation are relative, the two existing standalone digits48 remain absolute and accepted output stays exact

The complete containing system SHALL retain source identity and owner-aware geometry checks. #### Scenario: Judge bracket shape

### Requirement: Underlined anchor treatment
The combined condensed candidate SHALL use black .65pt underlines with a consistent4.6pt length centered on the musical note position, independent of the digit's ink width or bearing and contained within the existing horizontal head footprint. Actual owner-linked ink bounds and vertical protection SHALL remain truthful. Candidate-only1 glyph seating SHALL leave its dominant vertical body slightly right of the musical axis, using half of Round80's font-derived leftward displacement, without moving musical timing/stems/underlines. Narrow anchor1 at60, busy anchors15–16 and the clearer examples9/11 SHALL retain honest clearance. The underline SHALL NOT introduce bracket side padding into horizontal spacing. Historical bracket profiles, .45pt underlines, glyph-bound .65pt underlines and Round79's standard4pt treatment SHALL remain addressable.

#### Scenario: Judge the complete candidate
- **WHEN** the operator opens the gesture-relative candidate
- **THEN** underlines mark played group references consistently across all64 written bars without disrupting temporal spacing

#### Scenario: Narrow or asymmetric digit
- **WHEN** an absolute anchor's digit has narrow or side-biased ink, as1 at60
- **THEN** its underline retains the same centered4.6pt length as other anchors rather than inheriting the glyph's bias, and font-derived glyph seating preserves the musical note axis

### Requirement: Combined optical revision
The studio SHALL provide one complete three-page candidate combining standard4.6pt underlines, dominant-stroke seating for1 and a slightly leftward B seat relative to the fixed underline, so B's rounded right edge receives more overhang than its straight left edge. Actual source membership, musical axes and painted bounds SHALL remain truthful.7/9 SHALL retain their current seats as controls. The resolved contour choice SHALL NOT require separate comparison cards. Optical acceptance SHALL NOT be claimed solely from bounding boxes, centroids or passing geometry checks.

#### Scenario: Judge the combined revision

- **THEN** the typography and density changes appear together in one complete score, without redundant comparison cards

### Requirement: Source-preserving condensed presentation
The condensed candidate SHALL target three real-engine pages with at most six written bars per system, retaining all64 written bars, original digits/font sizes/register, rhythms, contexts, final0, repeats, expressions and current centered marks. Production admission SHALL remain active and use shared actual protected-head width evidence for supported ordinary/underlined heads. Existing chord/grace/tie/fold/repeat/barline reserves SHALL remain honest. Expanded markers or unsupported protection SHALL retain conservative fallback. Global defaults, the historical absolute Gold profile and historical Round79 SHALL remain unchanged; the accepted Round81 No14 publication uses this opt-in presentation.

#### Scenario: Compact sequential figures
- **WHEN** the condensed layout admits an ordinary six-eighth bar
- **THEN** equal-duration attacks retain equal temporal spacing at a shorter common scale, with no sequential parity shifts or glyph shrink

#### Scenario: Repeat entrance within a system
- **WHEN** repeat33 occurs inside a denser system
- **THEN** the complete repeat symbol and its first attack retain their required clearance and original repeat semantics

### Requirement: Quiet opening and coherent complete slurs
The new candidate SHALL evaluate both feasible slur sides with soft preferences for a quiet opening and a healthy complete curve. Source-steep spans SHALL NOT automatically exempt an anemic entry from aesthetic consideration. The selected route SHALL remain a coherent single bow with truthful clearance and source affiliation. Natural economical connections SHALL remain available, including the successful below route56; the successful steep above route43 SHALL be retained as a control. No measure whitelist, unconditional side rule, enlarged retained pools or fine two-dimensional runtime raster search SHALL be introduced. Historical optical-silhouette behavior SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Close side alternatives
- **WHEN** both sides admit comparable healthy curves
- **THEN** the side with a quieter musical-ink opening receives a soft preference, without losing independent clearance or useful natural endpoint connection

### Requirement: Source ending and selected-edition expression
The current candidate SHALL display the final written rests from the existing source-silence events, retaining their exact order and durations. It SHALL omit the encoded piano indicators at the opening and written33 under explicit source-linked corrections, retaining the original encoding facts and correct written/performed mappings. Crescendos/diminuendos and other source obligations SHALL remain intact. The operator's Henle observation SHALL be recorded as operator evidence, not misrepresented as an agent's full Henle comparison.

#### Scenario: Final written silence
- **WHEN** the last LH eighth ends in64
- **THEN** its authored eighth, eighth, quarter and eighth rests are visible in order, and the RH full-bar rest retains its authored identity

#### Scenario: Added piano indicators
