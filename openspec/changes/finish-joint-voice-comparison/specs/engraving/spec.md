## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Joint concurrent voice courses
The joint reading SHALL plan concurrent same-direction rhythmic voices together while retaining distinct primary rails and independent duration-level corridors. It SHALL preserve musical identities, source slurs and tie obligations, hand authority and onset seating. Control and historical options SHALL remain accessible with truthful source/engine claims.

#### Scenario: Co-moving independent voices
- **WHEN** concurrent same-direction voices move through the No. 13 complete gesture
- **THEN** their courses have coherent slopes and separate rhythmic statements, including unequal continuations and the tied approach

#### Scenario: Ordinary source slurs
- **WHEN** joint beams change the obstacle field
- **THEN** the two ordinary source slurs retain endpoints, provenance and prescribed sides without becoming pitch ties or extra duration strokes

#### Scenario: Retained default and history
- **WHEN** the joint reading is delivered for comparison
- **THEN** the historical Round58 options remain retrievable, the control uses current corrected source, and default/reference adoption is separately identified
