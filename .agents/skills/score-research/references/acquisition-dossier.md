# Acquisition handoff (fill only verified fields)

- **Observed on / requested decision:** Date; composer, work, movement/number, key, instrument, aliases; target edition selected by operator or proposed conditionally on source/critical quality evidence, independently of starter encoding parentage or availability. Henle Urtext can be a strong candidate, not an automatic winner by label.
- **First wave / search / coverage:** Pursue editorial-quality evidence and encoding discovery in parallel with actual page access for *every requested piece*. Include PDF attachments and institutional static original images/frames, not only catalogue PDFs; check intervening pages and report gaps before fallback choices. Use [source-route-record.md](source-route-record.md) for dated per-piece page states and independent reference/starter anchors. Venues and queries; full PDFs retrieved versus sample PDF, catalogue metadata or unpurchased offer; inspected direct files and bounded pages/measures or byte ranges; inaccessible items and failure modes; stop reason. Seek the highest-quality editorial edition first; record legitimate digital access (free or paid), cost gaps and preview limits without silently downgrading the target to a convenient encoding's parent or weaker accessible edition. Document reasons before considering an exceptional physical purchase/rescan; research does not authorize purchase.
- **Shortlist (one row per genuinely distinct route):** Item link; direct PDF/image/file link; publisher/editor/year/edition/plate/ISMN if known; witness/parent edition; format/extent/version/coverage; observed access versus completeness and human-preview readability; license claim versus verified reuse/redistribution permission; observed commit/hash only when bytes verified. Keep edition authority, transcription reliability, format convenience, rights and visual fidelity as separate judgments.
- **Fixed reference and passage checks:** Once accessible, name the provisionally fixed PDF or original-image sequence of the quality-first target and rationale; compare multiple available candidate score references against it, navigating independently. If unavailable, record the gap and any sampled alternatives without treating them as the selected target. Log beginning/end/critical or apparatus passages actually inspected, PDF pages separately from printed bars, known editorial differences and contradictory metadata; observation versus inference versus unknown. Do not count copied encodings as independent sources or viewing as whole-score certification.
- **Recommendation / decision gate:** Stop with verified per-piece reference coverage and a viable quality-first route, or a concrete edition/access/rights decision or bounded failure. HTTP success, PDF page counts and inventory metadata never establish human coverage. Name the quality-first comparison target, starter and corroborating witness as separate choices with limitations; starter parentage informs discrepancy checks, rights and correction effort, not target selection. Evaluate/correct or replace the starter against the selected target only in separately authorized later work. Record operator edition/access/rights or reading choice needed; next separately scoped certification/import task. Acquisition sampling is not whole-score certification; full musical/notation and separate visual checks remain open.
- **Live studio source-comparison handoff:** After legitimate access, plan separately authorized provisioning of actual operator-selected edition score pages beside the proposed transcription/starter in the existing studio Candidates view, subject to rights and tool support. Record the real studio URL and verified piece/page cues; state whether the selected-edition pages and starter are actually available and currently served, or whether preview remains unverified. Links alone or another edition's pages relabeled as the target do not count. If blocked by access, rights or tool support, state the blocker and next necessary action; do not claim acquisition or operator approval. Research does not authorize purchase, code changes, provisioning or import, and preview is not whole-score certification.
