# Acquisition handoff (fill only verified fields)

- **Observed on / requested decision:** Date; composer, work, movement/number, key, instrument, aliases; target edition chosen by whom or proposed conditionally.
- **Search/coverage:** Venues and queries; inspected direct files and bounded pages/measures or byte ranges; inaccessible items and failure modes; stop reason.
- **Shortlist (one row per genuinely distinct route):** Item link; direct file link; publisher/editor/year/edition/plate/ISMN if known; witness/parent edition; format/extent; observed access versus completeness; license claim versus verified reuse/redistribution permission; observed version/commit or hash only when verified. Keep musical authority, transcription reliability, encoding convenience and visual fidelity as separate judgments.
- **Passage checks and conflicts:** Beginning/end/critical or apparatus passages actually inspected; edition variants and contradictory metadata; observation versus inference versus unknown. Do not count copied encodings as independent sources.
- **Recommendation / decision gate:** Comparison target, starter and corroborating witness with limitations; operator edition/access/rights or reading choice needed; next separately scoped certification/import task. Acquisition sampling is not whole-score certification; full musical/notation and separate visual checks remain open.
