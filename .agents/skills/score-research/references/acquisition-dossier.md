- **Search/coverage:** Venues and queries; full PDFs retrieved versus sample PDF, catalogue metadata or unpurchased offer; inspected direct files and bounded pages/measures or byte ranges; inaccessible items and failure modes; stop reason. Find the best legitimately accessible scanned/digital edition (free or paid) before considering an exceptional physical purchase/rescan, with reason.
- **Shortlist (one row per genuinely distinct route):** Item link; direct PDF/file link; publisher/editor/year/edition/plate/ISMN if known; witness/parent edition; format/extent/version/coverage; observed access versus completeness and human-preview readability; license claim versus verified reuse/redistribution permission; observed commit/hash only when bytes verified. Keep edition authority, transcription reliability, format convenience, rights and visual fidelity as separate judgments.

Log beginning/end/critical or apparatus passages actually inspected, PDF pages separately from printed bars, known editorial differences and contradictory metadata; observation versus inference versus unknown. Do not count copied encodings as independent sources or viewing as whole-score certification.
- **Recommendation / decision gate:** Comparison target, starter and corroborating witness with limitations; operator edition/access/rights or reading choice needed; next separately scoped certification/import task.
