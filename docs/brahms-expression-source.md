# Brahms Op. 118/1 expression witness (Phase 1, source only)

`npm run brahms:export-expressions` compiles the pinned `data/sources/brahms-op118-no1/` transcription twice, with `\keepWithTag layout` and `\unfoldRepeats`. The offline listener is installed on four named Voices and two separately named Dynamics contexts. It records compiler `absolute-dynamic-event`, `crescendo-event`, `decrescendo-event`, and `sustain-event` at their *actual* moment, without finding a nearby note. `-- --check` compares the deterministic sidecar byte-for-byte and fails if LilyPond is absent. Source hashes are checked against the pinned manifest. Times are rational whole-note fractions; integer ticks are exact at 192 ticks/whole. The `origin.order` is listener order in context traversal; ordered same-tick `\sud` off/on events remain separate. `occurrence` counts repetitions of the same source location and context through LilyPond's unfolded repeats/alternatives. For `\sud`, LilyPond reports the two event origins in `global-variables.ily:126`, its macro definition; the event's performed moment, context and occurrence disambiguate each invocation. The parts source itself is unmodified.

Normalized layout inventory: **89 paint-eligible dynamic records** (28 literal marks: 16 `sf`, 11 `f`, one `p`; 59 closed hairpins: 25 crescendo, 34 decrescendo; two appearances of literal text `\cresc`). **Two additional diagnostic-only boundaries** preserve the redundant stop at parts line 383 column 10 on both unfolded occurrences. **116 source pedal events** (80 down, 36 up): seven actual ordered same-tick off/on pairs from `\sud`. The score-level overlay collapses exactly those seven pairs to `sustain-change` (109 overlay entries: 73 down, 29 up, seven change), each with both original off/on origins and their order. The sidecar retains all 116 constituent events. Paired layout `\sd` / MIDI `\sud` emits only a layout down. A repeated down does not imply a change. Every up (including the off half of a change) must have an active prior down; orphan ups at any unfolded occurrence fail with source location, time, bar and occurrence instead of being accepted as overlays. The seven literal off/on pairs occur at ticks 432, 816, 2352, 2736, 3888, 7728 and 13440. Generic LilyPond `\!` reports as a crescendo stop even for a decrescendo; the active spanner supplies the closed type. The repeat of `s8\!` at line 383:10 follows another explicit stop at line 382:69, and has no active span: it is retained as a diagnostic-only `redundant-stop` boundary (no invented duration), **only** at that specific source position and the two compiler-confirmed performed ticks, when its preceding line 382:69 stop actually closed the span 48 ticks earlier. All other unmatched stops throw origin-rich errors. The real-compiler microfixture independently confirms that adjacent literal `\!` commands emit separate, timed stop events. Boundaries are not copied to `score.dynamics`. Standalone text `\cresc` at line 371 is distinct from a hairpin and does not claim an endpoint. These records do not paint.

Intentionally excluded from this narrowly scoped source expression sidecar: text `espressivo` and other text scripts; mixed `dim.`/`rit.` spanners, tempo and agogic changes; layout engraver overrides, articulations and hand/voice correction. Neither the MIDI nor velocity, pedal synthesis, notes, ties or rendered ink are changed. `score.dynamics` and `score.pedals` expose this data as score-level overlays for a separately gated notation phase; `QuantizedNote.dynamicMark` is not inferred. The source witness is a transcription authority, not a claim of editorial completion.
