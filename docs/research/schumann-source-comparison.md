# Schumann Op. 68 Nos. 13, 14, 30, 43 — source-review handoff

The Candidates source comparison uses **original host JPEGs** (not copied into the repository) and one **published starter transcription PDF** in the operator's private development cache. The reference edition is Henle HN 45 (Ernst Herttrich) where the publisher's public preview covers the complete piece: No. 13, printed pp. 14–15 (preview frames 28–29), and No. 43, printed p. 59 (frame 73). The [official catalogue](https://www.henle.de/en/Album-for-the-Young-op.-68/HN-45) supplies the images. The publisher withholds one page each of Nos. 14 and 30; those withheld pages are neither retrieved nor represented here.

For Nos. 14 and 30 the selected accessible fallback is the **December 1848 Schuberth & Co first issue, plate 1232**, [Brahms-Institut ABH 5.2.187](https://brahmsinstitut.de/Archiv/web/bihl_digital/schumann_drucke_units/schum_op_068.html), printed pp. 16–17 and 38–39 respectively. No. 30 starts below the preceding work on p. 38. This historical witness is not a claim of definitive readings; Henle's critical apparatus remains relevant.
