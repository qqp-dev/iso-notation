## Decision and stop gate

**Recommendation, not final selection:** Human-preview the two retrievable scans first, using the **1897 first edition provisionally fixed** against both published transcription PDFs; switch to #10496 explicitly if it proves the better reference. The [second complete scan, IMSLP #10496](https://vmirror.imslp.org/files/imglnks/usimg/6/62/IMSLP10496-Scriabin_-_Op.11.pdf) was retrieved on 2026-09-26 (1,947,226 bytes, 40 PDF pages, SHA-256 `ac0f2ad9ef82cf06c862bfb1b3fb47554cbaf26754086e5977600b53ece6e9d3`); publisher unidentified, MIT archive provenance. Its No. 1 page range/legibility are also unverified. Henle HN 484 remains a valuable possible modern critical comparison, with preface/comments available; full score PDF access unverified. Alfred/Kalmus offers a paid individual PDF, **not purchased or inspected**. IMSLP #03773 is catalogue metadata only, **PDF not retrieved**. Evaluate Snortum and Mutopia side by side with a fixed reference before picking any transcription; Mysterium can flag discrepancies. Henle's [Urtext guidance](https://www.henle.de/en/What-is-Urtext/) explains why editorial source evaluation and choices matter beyond a label. No candidate was certified as an edition-faithful replica. No vetted MusicXML emerged **in this bounded search**; that is not a claim none exists.
