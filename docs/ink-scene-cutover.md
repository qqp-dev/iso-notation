# Placed painted-ink cutover (partial, version 3: fixed-core stored rest paint)

`buildInkScene` in `src/render/janko/ink-scene.ts` is built from the final `JankoSystemLayout` **after** slot shifts and any content-aware re-layout. Its key includes the complete input score (including source/editorial data), placed layout, resolved options and tokens, and the explicitly pinned shipped URW Gothic Demi face. There is no scene cache keyed by system count/first measure. The glyph contours are flattened from the pinned OTF; custom font families refuse measured coverage. Preliminary solver enquiries use the *same* `pitchGridRules` constructor via `preliminaryStaffRules`, not the final placed scene.

Fixed-core pitch-grid rules, measure barlines, beat pulses, ordinary halo/mask/digit noteheads **and short/continuous ledger rules** use scene primitive constructors in production paint. `elements/barlines.ts` adapters serialize their shared strokes; the fixed-core engine serializes placed scene pieces, preserving SVG grouping and paint order. `drawnStaffRuleYs`/`drawnStaffRuleBands` use the shared x-bounded rule constructor; detached seats, contour audits and tie-axis/tie-fusion checks inspect only segments reaching their x span, not an infinite rule through an empty measure. `sceneInkAt` is narrow-phase for **these families only**: dashed strokes and dashed white erase channels have finite painted intervals (their gaps cannot erase prior ink), hollow rings and glyph counters are empty, masks erase earlier ink and never occupy ink themselves. `scene.physical` clips strokes in **both axes** through painted erase intervals (including partially covered stroke widths) and gives finite broad boxes for this migrated slice; glyph/ring broad boxes still require narrow-phase testing. Migrated SVG serialization reads each stored scene primitive, not an independently retained original constructor input: mutation of a primitive moves its paint and its narrow-phase ink query together. `scene.physical` is a construction-time inventory; after editing a scene fixture call `scenePhysicalBoxes` again. White digit-mask rectangles are erase/readability policy, never physical ink, and `protects: 'own-digit'` names intent rather than certifying full containment. A collapsed head's semantic owners include its survivor and all `unisonMerges.mergedIds`, including exact merges without a surviving rhythm voice. `requireSceneCoverage` refuses absent families.

**Ledger authority:** `placedLedgerRules` constructs fixed-core short ledger and expanded equator rules (including two-rule channel boundaries), continuous outlier rules and short-rule suppression inside continuous spans. `ledgerPieces` adds survivor/merged note owners and structural span owners; the actual scene primitives serialize in production and supply `sceneLedgerAt` and finite `sceneLedgerBoxes`, with later head masks removing visible intervals. Ottava placement/lint use the shared ledger constructor rather than independently re-expanding equators/outlier spans. The non-fixed twin-row paint/page reservation remains labelled legacy. Existing conservative centre ±0.38pt ledger terms in `systemFurnitureBounds`, `systemCompleteInkBounds`, `checkSystemSlotFit` and `systemInkExtents` remain **page-policy reservations** until page placement and linter migrate together; they do not constitute physical ledger occupancy.

**Rest SVG authority (paint only):** The fixed-core `renderNotesLayer` now emits `sceneRestSvg` groups from final placed `InkScene.restPaint` records, between brackets and strict grid/head masks. Each record holds one original normalized `JankoRestInk` primitive, original order/group/class/layer, stable system/rest identity, hand, tick/duration/silence span and optional authored/source-silence origin; inferred gaps never borrow a neighbouring note owner. `placedRestPaint` constructs the glyph **once** when the scene is built; `serializeRestPaint` serializes those stored primitives, not the layout geometry or an SVG parse. The old independent fixed-core `renderRest` paint branch is removed. Non-fixed-core and standalone test callers retain `renderRest` solely as a sunset compatibility adapter over the **same** constructor and serializer; remove it when non-fixed dialects migrate to placed paint. `paintCoverage.stored` is typed separately from `coverage.migrated`: SVG emission is **not** a true-ink point/box solver.

**Incomplete physical coverage / labelled fallbacks:** `systemPaintedInkBoxes` inventories fixed-core ledger from the scene, retains `legacy broad …` bounds for stems/beams, brackets, ottava, handprint clusters and margin furniture, and explicitly labels written rests as conservative **page booking/admission** reservations, despite that method's historical name. No rest physical query or rest physical coverage is available; `requireSceneCoverage(scene, 'rests')` refuses it. Do not add rests to `orderedPieces`, `sceneInkAt` or `scenePhysicalBoxes`; a rest reservation is neither proof of filled ink nor free space. `restAdmissionBox` delegates unchanged to `restInkBox` and remains the **single conservative policy constructor** for pre-layout rest seats, beam, hold/detached/tie, ottava, lint and page booking; it is not a second paint or physical authority. Ties/holds/exception duration, guidelines, Middle C treatment, contour, compression and page policy have their own existing consumers and are not global scene occupancy. Their active clearances in the solver/linter remain explicitly legacy, including dot/hold/ottava and page/crop context; raw `pitchGridRules` readers already use the same structural constructor, but page/crop/global framing are not scene physical queries. The linter's content-aware page diagnostic uses `systemPageBookingBoxes`: a **named page-slot booking constraint**, preserving the former grid-envelope/head-reservation policy and diagnostics, never physical collision ink. `systemCompleteInkBounds`, `linter.systemInkExtents`, `paintedFacingGap`, global slot solving and crops are intentionally unchanged. Do not use the partial aggregate as global ink clearance.

**Pinned-font policy conflict (not promoted):** The shipped Demi face's uppercase `A` has advance 740 units and ink x=[7,733] (1000 UPM). At golden 5.8pt its centered ink extends ±2.8072pt, 0.0772pt farther each side than the current `snug` white knockout wx=2.73pt. The pre-existing generic 0.25em digit-width proxy does not represent this glyph. The placed scene records the real outline and leaves the judged SVG/mask unchanged; changing digit protection, GOLD spacing or linter gate requires a separate Candidate/performer legibility judgment rather than an invisible mask enlargement here. A real Bach placed `A` fixture confirms its measured horizontal ink extends beyond both mask edges; the partial scene does not prove a foreign stroke presently contacts that overhang. Full protection is therefore **not claimed**. Any collision/readability policy change requires a later real-engine Candidate judgment; this phase does not enlarge GOLD's mask.

**Rest sunset (next dedicated rest-authority solver stage, not permanent dual authority):** Six rest primitive families (`line`, cubic `curve`, open/closed `poly`, cubic `path`, `rect`, `ellipse`) across five styles/seven values block complete coverage. Dashed phantom hollow bars/ellipses, rounded and square hollow corners, cubic fills, open shapes and miter joins defeat the experimental sample-grid interval test (a real on-dash quarter-ellipse rim grazing a ~0.00004pt-wide box was missed); broad boxes and approximate points cannot certify painted-set emptiness. The independent grazing witness is an ellipse `rx=ry=2`, stroke `0.8`, and box x relative to centre `[2.39988, 2.39992]`, y `[-.039, .039]`, plus matched off-dash and just-outside negatives. Any unsupported case returns typed `unknown` with shape, owner and reason and refuses physical admission/promotion; neither a sample grid nor a broad bound may coerce unknown to clear/ink. Independently derive positive/negative SVG witnesses for hollow centres, dash on/off/opposite edge, grazing rims/just outside, square/rounded corners, cubic fills, open/closed polygons, round curves/miters and ordered erasure before retiring remaining admission/actual-ink legacy consumers. Recheck Bach GOLD/Brahms BRONZE page/crop/diagnostic parity. Until then no rest physical scene claim.

Retire each remaining labelled legacy bound only after that family's paint, placement clearance and lint oracle consume common primitives with parity fixtures; retire page booking separately when all families and page-policy constraints have complete coverage and independently pinned page/crop containment. Do not interpret absent scene coverage as a free seat.
