# Placed painted-ink cutover (partial, version 1)

`buildInkScene` in `src/render/janko/ink-scene.ts` is built from the final `JankoSystemLayout` **after** slot shifts and any content-aware re-layout. Its key includes the complete input score (including source/editorial data), placed layout, resolved options and tokens, and the explicitly pinned shipped URW Gothic Demi face. There is no scene cache keyed by system count/first measure. The glyph contours are flattened from the pinned OTF; custom font families refuse measured coverage. Preliminary solver enquiries use the *same* `pitchGridRules` constructor via `preliminaryStaffRules`, not the final placed scene.

Fixed-core pitch-grid rules, measure barlines, beat pulses and ordinary halo/mask/digit noteheads use scene primitive constructors in production paint. `elements/barlines.ts` adapters serialize their shared strokes; the fixed-core engine serializes placed scene pieces, preserving SVG grouping and paint order. `drawnStaffRuleYs`/`drawnStaffRuleBands` use the shared x-bounded rule constructor; detached seats, contour audits and tie-axis/tie-fusion checks inspect only segments reaching their x span, not an infinite rule through an empty measure. `sceneInkAt` is narrow-phase for **these families only**: dashed strokes and dashed white erase channels have finite painted intervals (their gaps cannot erase prior ink), hollow rings and glyph counters are empty, masks erase earlier ink and never occupy ink themselves. `scene.physical` clips strokes in **both axes** through painted erase intervals (including partially covered stroke widths) and gives finite broad boxes for this migrated slice; glyph/ring broad boxes still require narrow-phase testing. Migrated SVG serialization reads each stored scene primitive, not an independently retained original constructor input: mutation of a primitive moves its paint and its narrow-phase ink query together. `scene.physical` is a construction-time inventory; after editing a scene fixture call `scenePhysicalBoxes` again. White digit-mask rectangles are erase/readability policy, never physical ink, and `protects: 'own-digit'` names intent rather than certifying full containment. A collapsed head's semantic owners include its survivor and all `unisonMerges.mergedIds`, including exact merges without a surviving rhythm voice. `requireSceneCoverage` refuses absent families.

**Incomplete coverage / labelled fallbacks:** `systemPaintedInkBoxes` retains `legacy broad …` bounds for ledger/outlier rules, stems/beams, brackets, rests, ottava, handprint clusters and margin furniture. Ties/holds/exception duration, guidelines, Middle C treatment, contour, compression and page policy have their own existing consumers and are not global scene occupancy. Their active clearances in the solver/linter remain explicitly legacy, including dot/hold/ottava and page/crop context; raw `pitchGridRules` readers already use the same structural constructor, but page/crop/global framing are not scene physical queries. The linter's content-aware page diagnostic uses `systemPageBookingBoxes`: a **named page-slot booking constraint**, preserving the former grid-envelope/head-reservation policy and diagnostics, never physical collision ink. `systemCompleteInkBounds`, `linter.systemInkExtents`, `paintedFacingGap`, global slot solving and crops are intentionally unchanged. Do not use the partial aggregate as global ink clearance.

**Pinned-font policy conflict (not promoted):** The shipped Demi face's uppercase `A` has advance 740 units and ink x=[7,733] (1000 UPM). At golden 5.8pt its centered ink extends ±2.8072pt, 0.0772pt farther each side than the current `snug` white knockout wx=2.73pt. The pre-existing generic 0.25em digit-width proxy does not represent this glyph. The placed scene records the real outline and leaves the judged SVG/mask unchanged; changing digit protection, GOLD spacing or linter gate requires a separate Candidate/performer legibility judgment rather than an invisible mask enlargement here. A real Bach placed `A` fixture confirms its measured horizontal ink extends beyond both mask edges; the partial scene does not prove a foreign stroke presently contacts that overhang. Full protection is therefore **not claimed**. Any collision/readability policy change requires a later real-engine Candidate judgment; this phase does not enlarge GOLD's mask.

Retire each labelled legacy bound only after that family's paint, placement clearance and lint oracle consume common primitives with parity fixtures; retire page booking separately when all families and page-policy constraints have complete coverage and independently pinned page/crop containment. Do not interpret absent scene coverage as a free seat. Candidate/operator judgment is required for any new legibility or musical meaning policy; this migration makes no golden promotion or PDF release.
