# Placed painted-ink cutover (partial, version 5: beamed-solo stored paint and bounded physical queries)

`buildInkScene` in `src/render/janko/ink-scene.ts` is built from the final `JankoSystemLayout` **after** slot shifts and any content-aware re-layout. Its key includes the complete input score (including source/editorial data), placed layout, resolved options and tokens, and the explicitly pinned shipped URW Gothic Demi face. There is no scene cache keyed by system count/first measure. The glyph contours are flattened from the pinned OTF; custom font families refuse measured coverage. Preliminary solver enquiries use the *same* `pitchGridRules` constructor via `preliminaryStaffRules`, not the final placed scene.

Fixed-core pitch-grid rules, measure barlines, beat pulses, ordinary halo/mask/digit noteheads **and short/continuous ledger rules** use scene primitive constructors in production paint. `elements/barlines.ts` adapters serialize their shared strokes; the fixed-core engine serializes placed scene pieces, preserving SVG grouping and paint order. `drawnStaffRuleYs`/`drawnStaffRuleBands` use the shared x-bounded rule constructor; detached seats, contour audits and tie-axis/tie-fusion checks inspect only segments reaching their x span, not an infinite rule through an empty measure. `sceneInkAt` is narrow-phase for **these families only**: dashed strokes and dashed white erase channels have finite painted intervals (their gaps cannot erase prior ink), hollow rings and glyph counters are empty, masks erase earlier ink and never occupy ink themselves. `scene.physical` clips strokes in **both axes** through painted erase intervals (including partially covered stroke widths) and gives finite broad boxes for this migrated slice; glyph/ring broad boxes still require narrow-phase testing. Migrated SVG serialization reads each stored scene primitive, not an independently retained original constructor input: mutation of a primitive moves its paint and its narrow-phase ink query together. `scene.physical` is a construction-time inventory; after editing a scene fixture call `scenePhysicalBoxes` again. White digit-mask rectangles are erase/readability policy, never physical ink, and `protects: 'own-digit'` names intent rather than certifying full containment. A collapsed head's semantic owners include its survivor and all `unisonMerges.mergedIds`, including exact merges without a surviving rhythm voice. `requireSceneCoverage` refuses absent families.

**Ledger authority:** `placedLedgerRules` constructs fixed-core short ledger and expanded equator rules (including two-rule channel boundaries), continuous outlier rules and short-rule suppression inside continuous spans. `ledgerPieces` adds survivor/merged note owners and structural span owners; the actual scene primitives serialize in production and supply `sceneLedgerAt` and finite `sceneLedgerBoxes`, with later head masks removing visible intervals. Ottava placement/lint use the shared ledger constructor rather than independently re-expanding equators/outlier spans. The non-fixed twin-row paint/page reservation remains labelled legacy. Existing conservative centre ±0.38pt ledger terms in `systemFurnitureBounds`, `systemCompleteInkBounds`, `checkSystemSlotFit` and `systemInkExtents` remain **page-policy reservations** until page placement and linter migrate together; they do not constitute physical ledger occupancy.

**Rest SVG authority (stored paint; narrow physical consumer below):** The fixed-core `renderNotesLayer` now emits `sceneRestSvg` groups from final placed `InkScene.restPaint` records, between brackets and strict grid/head masks. Each record holds one original normalized `JankoRestInk` primitive, original order/group/class/layer, stable system/rest identity, hand, tick/duration/silence span and optional authored/source-silence origin; inferred gaps never borrow a neighbouring note owner. `placedRestPaint` constructs the glyph **once** when the scene is built; `serializeRestPaint` serializes those stored primitives, not the layout geometry or an SVG parse. The old independent fixed-core `renderRest` paint branch is removed. Non-fixed-core and standalone test callers retain `renderRest` solely as a sunset compatibility adapter over the **same** constructor and serializer; remove it when non-fixed dialects migrate to placed paint. `paintCoverage.stored` is typed separately from `coverage.migrated`: SVG emission alone is **not** a true-ink point/box solver.

**First certified rest-local consumer (filled rectangles only):** `rest-physical.ts` reads the stored `scene.restPaint` primitives through the same two-decimal `f` projection as the SVG serializer. It certifies a whole rest only when every primitive is a positive-area, unrounded, undashed filled rectangle without stroke, erasure or extra attributes. Point, positive-area closed box and pre-head-knockout disc queries return typed `ink` (piece/witness), `clear` (distance certificate) or `unknown` (piece/shape/reason). Boundary contact, disc tangency, nonfinite/zero-area input and unsupported shapes refuse. The live `checkRestClearance` rest→head branch uses this painted distance plus the independently retained named minimum readability air; it no longer measures `restAdmissionBox` for certified records. Unknowns fall back to the existing conservative admission box and are counted by reason in `LintReport.stats.restPhysical` (also preserved across combined studio reports). The protected-barline check still uses the original booking box. Current strict CLI census: Bach 0 certified/579 unsupported rest-head pairs, Brahms 0/1233, literal seven-value rest specimen 30/123; only the specimen currently exercises the certified half/whole cut. This is a real live checker retirement on those 30 decisions, **not** a claim of new global physical coverage or a golden appearance change.

**Grouped-beam cutover:** Final `layout.beams` produces ordered, owner-tagged fixed-core stem strokes, filled four-vertex rail paths (all levels and partial stubs), and filled augmentation dots. `sceneBeamSvg` reads the stored pieces in rhythm-layer order; `renderBeamGroup` remains only a thin non-fixed/standalone adapter to the same constructor and serializer. The grouped branch of `systemPaintedInkBoxes` now uses placed scene inventory, not the duplicated primary connector or broad stem reconstruction; non-fixed twin-row booking remains named legacy. `checkDotCountAgreement` counts placed dots, never re-renders the group; its independent expected musical duration and stem/rail connection assertions remain separate. `sceneInkAt` tests actual filled polygons/discs/stem widths and ordered strict-grid air and later head erasures. `sceneBeamIntersectsBox` clips the positive-area query to polygon/circle/stem and subtracts later erase intervals, refusing zero-area boxes. Scene physical rectangles are **broad-phase inventory**, especially for sloped rail corners: use the narrow queries for collisions. Stem-to-head and stem-to-rail joining is intentional attachment, never a foreign overlap. Group tick/span and note contributors do not replace timing or source provenance; preliminary beam-rest booking and its conservative lint policy are not placed rest ink.

**Beamed solo cutover:** Fixed-core final `layout.ungrouped` paints a single ordered stored `scene.solos` record for each visible beamed-dialect member, after suppression, handprint omission, chord-carrier duration and clasp-owned golden grammar. The record holds source/performed contributor IDs, tick, system, page piece, engraved duration and grammar. The same stored stem, white-filled/stroked ring, cubic path and dot primitives serialize in production and furnish scene inventory. `renderFlags` is now a thin direct-call adapter to that constructor; non-fixed `renderRhythm`, angled cuts and horizontal ticks remain explicitly legacy. `checkDotCountAgreement`/`checkStemRingGeometry` read stored marks instead of re-rendering; their independent expected counts come from engraved duration, and ring-to-head plus stem-axis/centre checks remain independent. The withdrawn historical Round 30 *adaptive Brahms complete-grammar* previews (`round-30-rings` mm. 1–2, 24–25; `round-30-double-dots` mm. 44–45; other measured contacts elsewhere in the full preview) now report seventeen real clasp-exception ring/mask contacts plus their two existing page-slot findings: the old audit incorrectly re-rendered all clasp members golden, even when production rendered complete exceptions. Note #5 at m. 1 lies in the parked rings window; its upper ring is genuinely clipped by its own later white mask. The literal SVG and scene point witnesses are unchanged; GOLD and active fixed-3 BRONZE are unaffected. The parked round is neither live Candidates nor judged/auto-promoted; current studio diagnostics remain unchanged, historical preview diagnostics are now honest and unjudged. No seat/musical change is inferred from this diagnostic alone. Fixed-core solo stem reconstruction was removed from `systemPaintedInkBoxes`; inventory now includes broad flag/ring/dot enclosures, labelled **nonphysical broad**. `sceneSoloAt` and `sceneSoloBoxAt` are *family-scoped*, never global occupancy: stem/dot/ring positive tests use emitted coordinates and ordered white ring clearing, strict-grid air and later head masks; cubic/evenodd path control hulls prove only outward clearance, with typed `unknown` near any lobe/counter/tangent. Finite positive-area boxes are required. This partial solver must not be promoted to `sceneInkAt` global free-space or a page/crop clearance gate; unmodelled families and later paint remain unsupported.

**Pre-final conservative policies, not physical scene queries:** first/second dot escape bounding envelopes (`engine.ts` preliminary seating and `linter.ts` dot readability audit) and ottava rest/flag candidate envelopes (`elements/ottava.ts`, `linter.ts`) remain named conservative placement/readability policies. They execute before final placed paint exists and preserve judged seats; next targeted sunset replaces their eligible shared projection without shifting GOLD. Other independent musical slot/stem tests are not scene self-comparisons.

**Incomplete physical coverage / labelled fallbacks:** `systemPaintedInkBoxes` inventories fixed-core ledger and grouped-beam pieces from the scene, retains `legacy broad …` bounds for non-beamed solo dialects, brackets, ottava, handprint clusters and margin furniture, and explicitly labels written rests as conservative **page booking/admission** reservations, despite that method's historical name. Only the narrow rest-local and family-scoped beamed-solo partial queries are available; global rest/solo physical coverage remains unavailable and `requireSceneCoverage(scene, 'rests')` refuses it. Do not add rests to `orderedPieces`, `sceneInkAt` or `scenePhysicalBoxes`; a rest reservation is neither proof of filled ink nor free space. `restAdmissionBox` delegates unchanged to `restInkBox` and remains the **single conservative policy constructor** for pre-layout rest seats, beam, hold/detached/tie, ottava, unsupported rest-head lint fallback, protected barlines and page booking; it is not a second paint or physical authority. Ties/holds/exception duration, guidelines, Middle C treatment, contour, compression and page policy have their own existing consumers and are not global scene occupancy. Their active clearances in the solver/linter remain explicitly legacy, including dot/hold/ottava and page/crop context; raw `pitchGridRules` readers already use the same structural constructor, but page/crop/global framing are not scene physical queries. The linter's content-aware page diagnostic uses `systemPageBookingBoxes`: a **named page-slot booking constraint**, preserving the former grid-envelope/head-reservation policy and diagnostics, never physical collision ink. `systemCompleteInkBounds`, `linter.systemInkExtents`, `paintedFacingGap`, global slot solving and crops are intentionally unchanged. Do not use the partial aggregate as global ink clearance.

**Pinned-font policy conflict (not promoted):** The shipped Demi face's uppercase `A` has advance 740 units and ink x=[7,733] (1000 UPM). At golden 5.8pt its centered ink extends ±2.8072pt, 0.0772pt farther each side than the current `snug` white knockout wx=2.73pt. The pre-existing generic 0.25em digit-width proxy does not represent this glyph. The placed scene records the real outline and leaves the judged SVG/mask unchanged; changing digit protection, GOLD spacing or linter gate requires a separate Candidate/performer legibility judgment rather than an invisible mask enlargement here. A real Bach placed `A` fixture confirms its measured horizontal ink extends beyond both mask edges; the partial scene does not prove a foreign stroke presently contacts that overhang. Full protection is therefore **not claimed**. Any collision/readability policy change requires a later real-engine Candidate judgment; this phase does not enlarge GOLD's mask.

Follow with brackets/bridges/holds and furniture, and migrate global page/crop/facing constraints only after every family is composed. A flag hull is not a permanent stand-in for path ink.

**Group consumer retirement and sunset:** Fixed-core grouped production paint, grouped legacy physical inventory and grouped-dot SVG re-render audit are retired. Angled-cut/horizontal-tick solo dialects, chord bridges, holds/duration, rests, contour and furniture do **not** inherit grouped coverage. The next coherent family is solo rhythm with ordered erasure; full six-shape rest-positive physical clearance remains mandatory before global rest coverage. `requireSceneCoverage` refuses every unmigrated family; no partial scene can certify global free space.
