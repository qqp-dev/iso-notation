No editable design sources or generation scripts for the OTF are identified in that tag. The OTF and these notices are included locally, not fetched at runtime. This is **not** the Bravura OFL font.

**Operator personal/offline integration only; external redistribution is not cleared.** The upstream exception names PostScript/PDF documents, not SVG data URLs or combined JavaScript software. Neither the preferred modifiable source for this font nor the effect of its AGPL on the combined ISO Practice bundle has been established. A bare source URL, an OTF copy and license text do not prove corresponding-source compliance. Separate source-form and licensing review is required before public release or wider redistribution. The operator declined font substitution for this personal-use phase; no ISO licensing choice or font change is made here. Do not publish the generated package in Git, Pages or releases.
