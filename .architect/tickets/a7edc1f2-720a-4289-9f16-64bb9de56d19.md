# Ticket: Tasteful Handedness Chevrons, Hold Line Obstacle Interruption, and Staff-Line Replacement

## Kind

## Problem

1. **Flawed Truncation in Measure 30 (Floating-Point Edge Cases & Relative Position)**:
   - In Measure 30, successive LH eighth notes (`bach-var1-501`, `504`, `507`, `510`, `513`) occur 24 ticks apart at physically close pitches.
   - The previous truncation check required `stem.y <= trailEndY`. When note release coincided with the subsequent note onset, floating-point rounding caused `stem.y <= trailEndY` to fail for notes like `bach-var1-501` and `bach-var1-507`. Consequently, the hold lines extended past the obstacle rather than cutting.
2. **Handedness Indicators: Tasteful Chevrons (`<` and `>`) Instead of Lateral Lines**:
   - The previous horizontal lateral stem lines (16pt long in print, 32px in canvas) were clumsy, traversed several pitch columns, and produced unwanted visual clutter and false collisions.
   - The user requested replacing them with tasteful `<` and `>` indicators:
     - LH hand-crossing exception (`hand === 'LH' && lPitch > 48`): `<` pointing left, placed on the left side of the notehead.
     - RH hand-crossing exception (`hand === 'RH' && lPitch < 48`): `>` pointing right, placed on the right side of the notehead.
     - Middle C (`lPitch === 48`) remains neutral (no indicator).
     - Chevrons must be tasteful, sleek, vertically centered at `ny`, with compact opening angle (~60°), rounded line caps/joins, matching duration color, and backed by a subtle white knockout halo so staff and beat lines do not cut through them.
3. **Obstacle Interruption ("Interrupt for the Obstacle then Finish")**:
   - In Measure 4, `bach-var1-68` (pitch 38, tick 552 held to 576) intersects the obstacle of `bach-var1-69` (tick 564 at pitch 36 with RH crossing). Under previous permanent truncation, the hold line stopped at tick 564 and never resumed, cutting the line too short to be useful.
   - Hold lines must not permanently terminate upon encountering an obstacle. Instead, they must **interrupt for the obstacle then finish**: segment the hold line to draw before the obstacle, break cleanly across the obstacle zone with whitespace padding, and continue after the obstacle to note release.
4. **Clean Colored Hold Rendering Over Staff Lines (Bar 6 Blue)**:
   - In Bar 6, `bach-var1-88` (tick 744, dur 36, blue) sits directly on staff line m2 (pitch 36, black octave line width 1.0pt).
   - In the previous commit, knockout underlays were removed and hold lines were stroked at `stroke-width="0.8"`. On staff lines, the narrower blue hold line was obscured or bordered by black artifacts from the underlying staff line.
   - When a note is on a staff line (`geom.isLine` is true), the colored hold line must cleanly draw over / replace the vertical staff line:
     - Apply a white knockout underlay along the hold segment (`stroke="#FFFFFF" stroke-width="${geom.isBold ? 2.5 : 1.8}" stroke-linecap="butt"`).
     - Stroke the colored hold line at full staff-line width (`stroke-width="${geom.isBold ? 1.35 : 1.0}" stroke-linecap="round"`), making the staff line cleanly turn the duration color (e.g. pure blue in Bar 6) with zero dark bleed.
     - Space notes continue to render solid thin hold lines (`stroke-width="0.8"`) without knockout.

### Solution

1. **Implement Tasteful `<` and `>` Chevrons in `src/render/print-layout.ts` & `src/render/score-canvas.ts`**:
   - For notes with hand-crossing exception (`(hand === 'RH' && rawLPitch < 48) || (hand === 'LH' && rawLPitch > 48)`):
     - Compute chevron dimensions: `w = 3.2pt`, `h = 5.2pt`, `clearance = 1.5pt` from notehead edge.
     - For LH (`<`):
       - `apexX = nx - halfW - clearance - w`, `apexY = ny`
       - `baseX = nx - halfW - clearance`, `topY = ny - h / 2`, `botY = ny + h / 2`
       - SVG path: `M ${baseX} ${topY} L ${apexX} ${apexY} L ${baseX} ${botY}`
     - For RH (`>`):
       - `apexX = nx + halfW + clearance + w`, `apexY = ny`
       - `baseX = nx + halfW + clearance`, `topY = ny - h / 2`, `botY = ny + h / 2`
       - SVG path: `M ${baseX} ${topY} L ${apexX} ${apexY} L ${baseX} ${botY}`
     - Underlay with white halo: stroke `#FFFFFF` with `stroke-width="2.2"`, then stroke `${noteColor}` with `stroke-width="0.8"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`, `stroke-linejoin="round"`.
     - In Canvas: draw the same chevron paths with `ctx.lineCap = 'round'`, `ctx.lineJoin = 'round'`, halo underlay, then noteColor.
2. **Implement Hold Line Obstacle Interruption**:
   - Collect obstacles in the column:
     - Each handedness chevron: X span `[min(baseX, apexX) - 1.5, max(baseX, apexX) + 1.5]`, Y span `[ny - h / 2 - 2.5, ny + h / 2 + 2.5]`.
     - Each notehead: X span `[nx - nw / 2 - 1.0, nx + nw / 2 + 1.0]`, Y span `[ny - nh / 2 - 2.0, ny + nh / 2 + 2.0]`.
   - For each held note (`durationTicks > tauRef`):
     - Initial interval: `[[trailStartY, trailEndY]]` where `trailEndY = Math.min(rawReleaseY, staffEndY)`.
     - For each obstacle (where `obs.noteId !== note.id`):
       - If `nx >= obs.x1 && nx <= obs.x2`:
         - Subtract `[obs.y1, obs.y2]` from all intervals in the list.
     - For each remaining segment `[segY1, segY2]` with `segY2 - segY1 >= 1.5`:
       - If `isOnStaffLine`:
         - Render white knockout line: `stroke="#FFFFFF" stroke-width="${geom.isBold ? 2.5 : 1.8}" stroke-linecap="butt"`.
         - Render colored hold line: `stroke="${noteColor}" stroke-width="${geom.isBold ? 1.35 : 1.0}" stroke-linecap="round"`.
       - If `!isOnStaffLine`:
         - Render colored hold line: `stroke="${noteColor}" stroke-width="0.8" stroke-linecap="round"`.
3. **Canvas Renderer Parity in `src/render/score-canvas.ts`**:
   - Implement identical obstacle collection, interval interruption, and staff-line knockout/widths for both vertical and horizontal layouts.
4. **Update Tests**:
   - Update `test/print-layout.test.ts` and `test/notation-variations.test.ts` to assert chevrons, multi-segment obstacle interruption in bar 4, clean truncation in measure 30, and staff-line replacement in bar 6.
