# Ticket: Round 6 — Single-Note Subdivisions (Classical & Modern) & Refined Hand-Cluster Clasps

## Kind

bounded

## Problem

The operator reviewed Round 5 and identified two fundamental refinements:

1. **Clasp Scope & Trigger (Hand-Cluster Only, Non-Vertical Only)**:
   - **Hand Boundary, Not Staff Boundary**: The clasp must be strictly per-hand (`RH` or `LH`). It must **never** combine left and right hands across the grand staff into a single mega-bracket. However, if a single hand cluster spans across Middle C (e.g. an octave+ stretch), its bracket spans that hand's full reach.
   - **Strictly for Non-Vertical Groups**: Notes that already align in a clean vertical column do NOT get a clasp. Single notes do NOT get a clasp. The clasp exists solely to unify horizontally displaced / row-snapped notes (such as the `2-5-9` cluster in Brahms m. 8).
   - **Duration Ownership**: The clasp carries the cluster's duration at its tip; noteheads inside do not carry redundant stems/flags.

2. **Single-Note Subdivision Representation (The 5 Candidates)**:
   - The current isolated single-note flags (curved bezier wire "bends") are visually clumsy.
   - The operator wants a balanced candidate matrix comparing **2 classical engraving traditions** and **3 creative modern concepts**:
     1. **Classical 1: Sculpted Calligraphic Urtext Flag** (`classical-urtext`): Tapered calligraphic burin curve with optical body weighting (Henle / Bärenreiter lineage).
     2. **Classical 2: Historic Copperplate Triangular Pennant** (`copperplate-pennant`): Straight-edged triangular wedge tapering off the stem tip (early European copperplate engraving).
     3. **Modern 1: Architectural Lateral Tab** (`architectural-tab`): Crisp horizontal tick-tabs perpendicular to the stem (1 tab for 8ths, 2 for 16ths; grid-aligned & architectural).
     4. **Modern 2: Beveled Burin Slash / Chevron** (`beveled-slash`): Sharp 45° beveled angled cuts on the stem tip, harmonized with the design system's French Guillemet handedness chevrons.
     5. **Modern 3: Modernist Aerodynamic Winglet** (`aerodynamic-winglet`): Sleek modern aerodynamic fin with a straight vertical spine and a sharp diagonal cutback.

3. **Strict Implementer Verification Boundary**:
   - Run ONLY `npm test`, `npm run lint:engraving --strict`, and `npm run build`.
   - Do NOT run unconfigured flags (e.g. `--noUnusedLocals`).
   - Keep diffs strictly minimal and bounded to the ticket requirements.

## Requirements

### 1. Types & Tokens (`src/render/janko/types.ts`)
- Add `subdivisionStyle?: 'classical-urtext' | 'copperplate-pennant' | 'architectural-tab' | 'beveled-slash' | 'aerodynamic-winglet'` to `JankoLayoutOptions`.
- Default in `DEFAULT_JANKO_OPTIONS`: `'classical-urtext'`.
- Ensure `chordGrouping` supports `'per-hand-clasp'` (or refines `left-clasp-spire` to be strictly per-hand on non-vertical clusters).

### 2. Clasp Refinement (`src/render/janko/elements/rhythm.ts` / `engine.ts`)
- In `computeClaspGeometry` / `renderChordClasp`:
  - Grouping unit is **per-hand** (`LH` or `RH`), determined by note onsets *within that hand*.
  - A clasp is generated **if and only if** the hand's onset contains $\ge 2$ notes AND at least two notes have distinct $x$ positions (i.e. horizontally spread / row-snapped, such as $\max(x) - \min(x) > 1.0\text{pt}$).
  - Spans from $\min(y) - r$ to $\max(y) + r$ of that hand's notes (including across Middle C if the hand's notes span across it).
  - Downbeat clasps maintain $\ge 3.5\text{pt}$ clearance from the barline (`claspMinBarlineAir`).
  - Clasp carries the cluster's duration using the active `subdivisionStyle` at the tip.

### 3. Single-Note Flag / Subdivision Renderers (`src/render/janko/elements/rhythm.ts`)
- Refactor `renderFlags` to dispatch to the active `subdivisionStyle`:
  1. `classical-urtext`: Fluid tapered burin hook with optical body thickness (filled path with curve and taper).
  2. `copperplate-pennant`: Sharp triangular pennant (`M stemX y L stemX+w y+h/2 L stemX y+h Z` filled or stroked wedge).
  3. `architectural-tab`: Clean horizontal rectangular tabs extending rightward from stem tip (`stemX` to `stemX + tabW`, thickness `1.1pt`).
  4. `beveled-slash`: 45° beveled slashes cutting through/across the stem tip, matching chevron weight.
  5. `aerodynamic-winglet`: Tapered fin with vertical spine and sharp angled cutback.
- Stacks for 16ths and 32nds: proper vertical spacing matching `flagSpacing`.
