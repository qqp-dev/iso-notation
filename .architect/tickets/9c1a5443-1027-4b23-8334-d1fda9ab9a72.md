# Ticket: Optical Notehead Balance, Thin Long Stems & Bulletproof A4 Print Pagination

## Kind

bounded — engraving visual tuning & print stylesheet fix: shrink circles to optically match diamonds, refine lateral stems to thin/long Klavar lines, style PC 4 with thin long strokes, and fix A4 @page CSS for exact 2-page pagination.

## Problem

Operator feedback from live inspection and test printing:
1. **Notehead Optical Balance**: Circles look significantly too large compared to diamonds due to area imbalance ($\pi r^2$ vs $2 d^2$, circle having +57% area when $r = d$). The circles must be shrunk to optically match the diamonds visually (e.g. diamond $d = 4.0\text{pt}$, circle $r = 3.2\text{pt}$ in print; matching area ratio on canvas).
2. **Line Geometry (Stems & Demarcation)**: The lines were "too thick and too short":
   - Lateral stems were short stubby nubs ($3.8\text{pt}$ visible, $1.2\text{pt}$–$1.6\text{pt}$ thick). They must be thin (`0.6pt` / `0.8px`) and gracefully long (extending $12\text{pt}$ / $18\text{px}$ from note center) so they read as crisp directional pointers.
   - Demarcation at PC 4 ($E$) must be a thin, long line (`0.5pt` / `0.6px`, either solid or long-dashed like Klavar `[12, 4]`, never stubby stippled dots).
3. **A4 Browser Print Paging Failure**: Printing from browser resulted in broken pagination because `@page { margin: 10mm; }` combined with `width: 210mm; height: 297mm` SVG forced vertical overflow and created extra blank/broken pages. Fix via `@page { size: A4 portrait; margin: 0; }` and strict `210mm × 297mm` `.print-page` containers with `page-break-inside: avoid; overflow: hidden;`.
4. **Consistency**: Ensure full visual agreement between the main canvas, the print modal preview, and the printed page.

## Testing plan

1. **Optical Notehead Sizing Invariant**:
   - In print SVG: Diamond $d = 4.0\text{pt}$, Circle $r = 3.2\text{pt}$.
   - Optical areas match within 5% ($\pi \cdot 3.2^2 \approx 32.2\text{ pt}^2$, $2 \cdot 4.0^2 = 32.0\text{ pt}^2$).
   - On canvas: proportional area balance maintained.
2. **Thin Long Lateral Stems Invariant**:
   - Stems are thin (`0.6pt` in SVG, `0.8px` on canvas).
   - Stems extend $\ge 12\text{pt}$ in SVG and $\ge 16\text{px}$ on canvas, projecting clearly past noteheads.
3. **Clean PC 4 Demarcation Invariant**:
   - PC 4 line is thin (`0.5pt` / `0.6px`) with long Klavar-style segments (`[12, 4]` or solid), zero short stubs.
4. **Bulletproof A4 Pagination Invariant**:
   - `@page` rule has `size: A4 portrait; margin: 0;`.
   - `.print-page` has explicit `width: 210mm !important; height: 297mm !important; max-height: 297mm !important; overflow: hidden !important;`.
   - Page 1 and Page 2 format cleanly as exactly 2 pages with zero page breaks or overflow.
5. **Verification**:
   - Automated tests in `test/notation-variations.test.ts` and `test/print-layout.test.ts`.
   - `npm test` passes cleanly.
   - `npm run build` succeeds cleanly.
