# Ticket: Aligned 5/7 Boundary Line at PC 4, Continuous Thin Klavar Line & Scaled-Up Noteheads

## Kind

bounded — notation engraving refinement: replace unaligned 4.5 line with aligned 5/7 demarcation at PC 4 (E), render thin continuous lines like Klavarscribo, and scale up both notehead types (circles and diamonds).

## Problem

Operator feedback on the live site:
1. **5/7 Line Alignment**: The previous iteration placed an unaligned line at PC 4.5. Instead, the 5/7 demarcation should replace the old tritone line (PC 6) and sit directly on the whole-tone grid at **PC 4 ($E$)**—the boundary line of the 5-group ($C, D, E$).
2. **Klavarscribo-Style Line**: Replace the short stippled dashes (`5, 4`) with a thin continuous, unbroken line (like Klavarscribo) so it reads as a clean structural line rather than points/dots.
3. **Notehead Sizing**: Both note types (discs and diamonds) should be slightly bigger (~20–25% increase, e.g. $r = d = 4.2\text{pt}$ in print, and scaled up on canvas), maintaining strict vertical equality ($d = r$) so ascending 16ths form a continuous straight line.
4. **Site Display Only**: Show changes in the live web app and print modal. Do NOT send print jobs to the printer.

## Testing plan

1. **Aligned 5/7 Demarcation Invariant**:
   - PC 0 ($C$) is the bold octave boundary line.
   - PC 4 ($E$) is the 5/7 demarcation line (replacing the old PC 6 tritone line), aligned at integer pitch 4.
   - PC 2 ($D$), PC 6 ($F\sharp$), PC 8 ($G\sharp$), PC 10 ($A\sharp$) are subtle hairlines.
   - Zero lines at non-integer pitch coordinates (no 4.5 floating line).
2. **Continuous Thin Line Invariant**:
   - Staff lines (including PC 4) are rendered as continuous solid lines (or long-segment strokes) without stippled point/dash patterns.
3. **Notehead Sizing Invariant**:
   - Both circle radius $r$ and diamond half-diagonal $d$ are scaled up to $\ge 4.2\text{pt}$ in print SVG and proportionally on canvas.
   - $d = r$ holds strictly: vertical top and bottom extents are identical ($[y - r, y + r]$).
4. **Verification**:
   - Automated unit tests covering PC 4 demarcation, zero 4.5 lines, notehead sizing, and lateral stems.
   - `npm test` passes cleanly.
   - `npm run build` succeeds cleanly.
