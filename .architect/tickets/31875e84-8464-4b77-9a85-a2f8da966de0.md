# Ticket: Metric Legibility & Comparison Toggles (Gould Beams, Klavar Beat Grid, Gutter Brackets)

## Kind

bounded — implemented and verified across canvas, SVG print layout, UI controls, and test suite.

## Problem

In proportional vertical isomorphic notation, running passages of equal duration (e.g. continuous 16th notes in Bach Goldberg Variation 1) can be cognitively challenging to parse into beats without metric visual aids.
To provide optimal player legibility and allow empirical evaluation of competing philosophies, we explored three distinct approaches:
1. **Elaine Gould Angled Beams**: Melodic contour-following connecting beams with uniform base stem lengths ($L_0 \ge 12\text{pt}$), slope capping ($\le 16\text{pt}$/beat), and subdivision stream filtering.
2. **Option 1: Klavarskribo Beat Grid**: Horizontal dotted pulse hairlines for intermediate beats (Beat 2, Beat 3) between solid measure barlines across staff pitch space, completely avoiding diagonal lines while clearly delineating spatial metric compartments.
3. **Option 2: Gutter Beat Brackets**: Vertical metric grouping brackets situated strictly in the outer margins/gutters (LH in left gutter, RH in right gutter) with beat numbers, preserving pitch space as 100% sacred and unadorned.

All three approaches are independently toggleable directly on the page and in the controls drawer so the player can effortlessly compare legibility in real time.

## Testing plan

1. **Option 1 (Klavarskribo Beat Grid) Invariants**:
   - In vertical canvas: renders fine dotted horizontal pulse lines (`setLineDash([2, 3])`, `0.6px`, `rgba(255,255,255,0.20)`) for beats $2 \dots N$ across the staff.
   - In SVG print layout: renders `<line stroke-dasharray="2,3" opacity="0.45"/>` at beat positions.
2. **Option 2 (Gutter Beat Brackets) Invariants**:
   - LH brackets render in the left margin (`staffLeft - 12`); RH brackets render in the right margin (`staffRight + 12`).
   - Brackets encompass the beat window with horizontal end caps (`capLen = 4`) and bold beat number labels.
   - Active beat brackets glow with bright gold (`#FACC15`, `2.0px`) during live playback.
   - SVG print engine renders corresponding `<path>` and `<text>` elements in column margins.
3. **Interactive UI Toggles On The Page**:
   - Floating header toolbar on the page features 1-click quick-toggle pill buttons: `🎶 Beams`, `📏 Beat Grid`, and `⊏ Brackets`.
   - Controls drawer under `Overlays` provides explicit checkboxes.
   - `PrintModal` dynamically inherits and passes active options to the SVG print engine.
