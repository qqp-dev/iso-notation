# Ticket: Top-Aligned Longer Stems, Outlier Pitch Preservation, Clean Landmarks (m1/m3/m5), and Permanent Klavar Beat Grid

## Kind

bounded

## Problem

Four notation details require refinement to achieve clean, authentic Klavarskribo-inspired player legibility:
1. **Stem Alignment & Length**: Lateral handedness stems currently slice through the vertical middle of noteheads (`cy`) and are somewhat short. In Klavarskribo, stems originate from the **top** of the notehead (the onset point) and extend further outward for clear peripheral hand recognition.
2. **Ugly Octave Up Black Blob**: Outlier notes above m5 (like high D6 in Bach Goldberg Var 1, pitch 74) currently get folded into octave 4 and obscured by an ugly black blob badge `↑8`. Since D6 is only 2 semitones above m5 (pitch 72), it comfortably fits in the right margin buffer without folding or badges.
3. **Octave Marker Redundancy**: The top header prints `m1`, `m2`, `m3`, `m4`, `m5`. `m2` and `m4` are visually redundant and obvious from context between `m1`, the authoritative `m3` Middle C axis, and `m5`.
4. **Unnecessary UI Toggles**: Gutter Brackets should be abandoned. The Klavarskribo dotted beat grid is our permanent standard design. No toggles are needed on the page, so the floating header should be decluttered.

## Klavarskribo Engraving Solution

1. **Top-Aligned Longer Lateral Stems**:
   - In vertical canvas (`src/render/score-canvas.ts`): Draw horizontal lateral stems aligned with the top edge of the notehead (`stemY = cy - noteHeight / 2` or onset tick coordinate), with length `stemLength = Math.max(20, options.pixelsPerSemitone * 1.45)`.
   - In SVG print layout (`src/render/print-layout.ts`): Align lateral stems with the note top edge (`stemY = ny - nh / 2`), with `stemLength = 16.0pt`.
   - Stems point Right for RH and Left for LH, clearly visible from peripheral vision.
2. **Outlier Pitch Preservation & Clean Thin Vector Octave Indicators (No Black Blobs)**:
   - For Bach Goldberg Var 1, the high D6 (pitch 74) fits within the 3-semitone symmetrical right margin buffer past m5 (pitch 72), so it renders directly at its true pitch on the right past the m5 line without folding or badges.
   - When octave displacement IS necessary (e.g. extreme registers or pieces where notes exceed the margin buffer and folding is required):
     - Completely eliminate the heavy black background rectangle/pill badge.
     - Render a clean, thin, elegant up-arrow and an 8 (`↑8` / `↓8`), styled as crisp, delicate vector text (`font-size="6pt"` in SVG, thin glyph in canvas) without any black blob background obscuring the note.
3. **Declutter Octave Markers (Drop `m2` and `m4`)**:
   - In both canvas (`score-canvas.ts`) and SVG print layout (`print-layout.ts`):
     - Print `m1` (bottom boundary), `m3` (Middle C center landmark), and `m5` (top staff boundary).
     - Drop `m2` and `m4` (when `displayOct === 2 || displayOct === 4`, skip rendering the header label and guide tick).
4. **Permanent Klavarskribo Beat Grid & Zero Toggles**:
   - `showBeatGrid` is permanently enabled (`true`) by default.
   - Remove the grouping toggle (`showGutterBrackets`) and the metric toggle button group entirely from the floating toolbar on the page (`src/ui/App.tsx`).
   - Remove the `showGutterBrackets` checkbox from `src/ui/ControlsDrawer.tsx`.
   - The page header remains clean, minimal, and uncluttered.
